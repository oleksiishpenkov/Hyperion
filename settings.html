Truck routes and time norms used by Dispatch, the driver app and order distances.

Truck routing

Distances and driving times come from a routing service that follows the real road network with your truck's size and weight, so it avoids low bridges and roads closed to trucks. Each route is asked once and saved.

Truck profile

Typical container combination: tractor with 40 ft chassis. Change it if your fleet differs.

Time norms

How long each part of a job takes on the Dispatch board. Driving time = distance ÷ average speed; the distance comes from the routing service when one is set up.

Driving and rest rules (EU Regulation 561/2006) checked on the Dispatch board, per driver:

  • at most 4.5 h of driving in a row, then a 45 min break (added to the job automatically);
  • driving in one shift: 9 h, up to 10 h no more than twice a week;
  • shift (from the first pick-up to the last drop-off, breaks included): 13 h, up to 15 h no more than three times a week;
  • rest between shifts at least 9 h; at most 56 h of driving a week.
  • when planning a long job: once the shift reaches the planned driving or shift limit, a daily rest is added and driving goes on in a new shift.

When drivers rest during loading or unloading, that time resets the 4.5 h count. The week runs Monday to Sunday. Hyperion only knows the jobs planned in it, not the tachograph, so hours driven elsewhere are not counted.
